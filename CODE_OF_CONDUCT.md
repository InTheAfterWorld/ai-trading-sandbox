# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience
for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity, experience, education, socio-economic status,
nationality, appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity.

## Our Standards

Be respectful and kind; give and accept constructive feedback.

Unacceptable: sexualized language, trolling, personal/political attacks,
public/private harassment, or publishing others' private info without
permission.

## Enforcement

Community leaders may remove, reject, or edit contributions that violate this
code and will take fair corrective action.

## Scope

Applies to all community spaces: the repo, issues, pull requests, discussions.

## Reporting

Report violations in confidence to the maintainers: message
[@LXTimer](https://github.com/LXTimer) or use GitHub's private reporting.
